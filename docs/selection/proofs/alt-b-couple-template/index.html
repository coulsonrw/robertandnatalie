Questions

Good to know

When should I respond?
Please respond by the date shown with your invitation. You can return to this website to update your response until that date.
Who is included in my invitation?
Your invitation is addressed to the members of your household. When you RSVP, the form shows exactly who is included, and you respond for each person by name.
How do I get from the chapel to the hotel?
The ceremony and reception are at two different venues. Directions for each are in the Wedding Day section above.
I have an access or dietary need. How do I let you know?
When you RSVP there is a short, optional notes field. Anything you share there is used only for planning and is passed only to the people who need it to look after you on the day. You are also welcome to contact us directly.
