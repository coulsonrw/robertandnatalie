# Weighted scorecard — design-foundation selection (PRD v1.1 TPL-07)

**Prepared:** 2026-09-21. **Prepared by:** implementation agent (recommends; does not approve). **Candidate IDs:** from `CANDIDATE_REGISTER.md`. **Scoring basis:** the PRD weights and anchors reproduced below; project decision rules, not popularity measures.

## Rules applied

| Criterion | Weight | Required basis (PRD TPL-07) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Fit with the approved invitation | 35 % | Actual crest and copy; ivory/gold/charcoal treatment; type hierarchy; ornamentation; fidelity without an image-only page. |
| 2. Mobile usability and guest navigation | 20 % | Readable 320/390 px layouts; clear schedule/travel navigation and RSVP entry; no mandatory story or animation. |
| 3. Technical fit and maintainability | 20 % | Inspected source/build; component structure; dependencies; integration path; owner-controlled deployment. |
| 4. Customization effort and total cost | 15 % | Keep/adapt/replace assessment, comparable work estimates, licensing and recurring costs, and uncertainty. |
| 5. Accessibility and performance readiness | 10 % | Measured proof behaviour, keyboard/reflow/reduced-motion checks, load evidence, costed remediation. |

**Anchors:** 0 = cannot meet the criterion on an acceptable path; 1 = very poor fit / major rebuild; 2 = substantial adaptation; 3 = workable with moderate changes; 4 = strong fit with limited changes; 5 = demonstrated fit with minimal relevant remediation.

**Calculation:** contribution = weight × score ÷ 5; total out of 100 = sum of contributions. Weights total 35 + 20 + 20 + 15 + 10 = **100** and are never renormalised. A criterion without verified evidence is left **BLANK** and its contribution is shown as a **possible range** with 0 and 5 as the only bounds (0 to the full weight). A total containing any blank is a **provisional total (range)** and **is not equivalent to, and must not be ranked against, a fully evaluated total**. A mandatory gate failure is a rejection regardless of score (TPL-05).

**Evidence basis by candidate (TPL-07 "actual branded proof where available"):**

- **C01** is scored on the working proofs in `docs/proofs/` (47 captures at `7f8d326`, 2026-09-21T23:11Z, Chromium 141.0.7390.37) and the audits in `docs/evidence/` (2026-09-21T23:16Z), plus the clean build reproduced on 2026-09-21 at HEAD `7f8d326`.
- **C36 (alt-a)** and **C37 (alt-b)** are scored on their **original concept mockups** in `docs/selection/proofs/` — labelled by their authors as *untested interactions, not a template implementation*. Per TPL-10 they are not credited with having passed interactive, accessibility or performance tests; criterion 5 is therefore BLANK for both.
- **External retained candidates (C03, C04, C05, C06, C24, C30, C31)** have no branded proof and, for the commercial items, no opened demo or source. All five criteria are BLANK for each; the "screening expectation" column repeats the register's screening note and is **not a score**.
