# Story sketch overlays

Edge-traced line-art SVGs produced with OpenCV / Potrace from the published
full-colour chapter photo derivatives in `assets/story/derivatives/`. They are
overlays only: the photographs themselves are unchanged and are not
AI-generated or re-touched.

Used on the public Our Story timeline for chapters 2–6 (scroll-draw, then
full-colour still). Chapters 1, 7–9 and 10 have no sketch.
